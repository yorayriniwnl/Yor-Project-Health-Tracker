# Report Design

All reports support date range, department, location, contractor, health status, and export format filters.

## Reports

1. Project Health Report: score and classification for all projects
2. Budget Overrun Report: budget spent above allocation or expected burn rate
3. Delayed Project Report: overdue projects and projects behind schedule
4. Contractor Performance Report: ranking by success rate, total delivered, on-time, within-budget, and rating
5. Broker Risk Report: broker involvement, compliance, conflict of interest, and risk level
6. Maintenance Cost Report: projects with high annual maintenance percentage
7. Department Performance Report: project count, budget usage, delays, and average health score
8. Public Transparency Report: public-safe fields only

## Export formats

- CSV for lightweight data exchange
- Excel for official analysis and pivot tables
- PDF for audit submissions and meeting packets
